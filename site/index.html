About Arin

Arin Handyman and Contracting takes on home repairs and renovation work for homeowners in Halton and the Greater Toronto Area, from single repairs to kitchen and bathroom updates.

Each job starts with a description of the work. From there we go over what’s involved, including anything that needs a permit, a licensed trade, or a closer look, before preparing an estimate.
